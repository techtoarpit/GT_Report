# Mid-Semester Review: Top 8 Talking Points

The 8 core quantitative findings from our empirical study of efficient Shapley value approximations, with one self-contained sentence each:

1. **0.0076 (0.76% Relative Error)**: Paired KernelSHAP achieves a remarkable relative approximation error of just $0.0076 \pm 0.0007$ at budget $m=2048$, delivering the lowest error among all five estimators across every single budget evaluated.
2. **62.8% Variance Reduction**: By pairing sampled coalitions with their exact set complements, Paired KernelSHAP reduces relative error by $62.6\%$ compared to standard KernelSHAP and by $62.8\%$ compared to Permutation sampling at $m=2048$.
3. **6.39x Computational Speedup**: Paired KernelSHAP matches the final accuracy of Permutation sampling ($m=2048$) with only $m^* = 321$ coalition evaluations, yielding an empirical $6.39\times$ reduction in required model evaluations.
4. **98.0% Top-3 Ranking Fidelity at m=512**: For practical feature selection, Paired KernelSHAP recovers the true top-3 most influential features with $98.0\%$ agreement at budget $m=512$ and reaches $99.7\%$ at $m=2048$, whereas standard Permutation and standard KernelSHAP fail to reach $99\%$ fidelity across the entire tested budget range.
5. **-0.563 Log-Log Convergence Slope**: All four sampling-based estimators exhibit empirical convergence slopes closely aligning with the theoretical $O(m^{-1/2})$ Monte Carlo limit ($\alpha_{\text{perm}} = -0.535$, $\alpha_{\text{paired}} = -0.563$), with Paired KernelSHAP slightly steeper due to exact linear efficiency projection.
6. **0.1103 Multilinear Efficiency Gap**: Unlike the other four estimators which enforce exact efficiency conservation to machine precision ($< 10^{-14}$), Multilinear Extension incurs a persistent efficiency gap of $0.1103 \pm 0.0100$ at $m=2048$ due to trapezoid quadrature discretization error on non-linear decision functions.
7. **1.32 s vs 3,800 Years (The Cost Wall)**: Computing exact Shapley values requires $409,600$ forward passes taking $1.32$ seconds per instance at $d=12$, but the exponential $2^d$ barrier drives exact calculation to $3.9$ minutes at $d=20$, $2.2$ days at $d=30$, and $3,800$ years at $d=50$, proving approximation is strictly mandatory for $d > 16$.
8. **93.57% Model Test Accuracy**: The HistGradientBoostingClassifier retrained strictly on the top-12 mutual-information features of the stratified 70/30 split achieves $93.57\%$ test accuracy (160/171 correct), confirming that explanations are evaluated on a high-performing, well-regularized predictive model.
